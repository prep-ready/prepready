In an emergency the pharmacy may be closed and an ambulance may take longer than usual. A well-stocked kit lets you treat cuts, burns and sprains yourself and get through a few days without a doctor. This list is based on the American Red Cross kit for a family of four, with additions from the Polish government's Safety Guide.

This guide does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. Ask a pharmacist about medication, especially for children, older people and anyone with a long-term condition.

## Red Cross list for a family of four

| Item | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Absorbent compress dressings (5 × 9 in) | 2 |
| Adhesive bandages, assorted sizes | 25 |
| Adhesive cloth tape (10 yd × 1 in) | 1 |
| Antibiotic ointment packets | 5 |
| Antiseptic wipe packets | 5 |
| Aspirin, 81 mg | 2 packets |
| Emergency blanket | 1 |
| Breathing barrier with one-way valve | 1 |
| Instant cold compress | 1 |
| Non-latex gloves, large | 2 pairs |
| Hydrocortisone ointment packets | 2 |
| Gauze roller bandage, 3 in | 1 |
| Roller bandage, 4 in | 1 |
| Sterile gauze pads, 3 × 3 in | 5 |
| Sterile gauze pads, 4 × 4 in | 5 |
| Oral thermometer (non-mercury, non-glass) | 1 |
| Triangular bandages | 2 |
| Tweezers | 1 |
| First aid instruction booklet | 1 |

## Worth adding for a crisis

The Polish Safety Guide adds:

- regular prescription medication, with a supply for several days,
- painkillers and anti-inflammatories,
- anti-nausea and anti-diarrhoea medication,
- burn dressings,
- a tourniquet and scissors,
- FFP3 masks.

## How to put it together

1. Go through what you already have. Return expired medication to a pharmacy rather than the bin.
2. Buy what is missing. A ready-made kit is a good start, but it usually needs more dressings and your own medication.
3. Pack everything in one waterproof bag or box, clearly labelled.
4. Add a card listing each person's medication, doses and allergies, plus the emergency number.
5. Keep it where everyone knows to look, not in a damp bathroom.
6. Put a smaller version in your [go-bag](/en/guides/go-bag-checklist/).

## Skills matter more than the kit

A first aid course teaches you how to stop bleeding, perform CPR, use the recovery position and treat burns. Learn to use a tourniquet on a course before you ever need one.

## What to check every few months

- expiry dates on medication and sterile dressings,
- packaging intact and dry,
- prescriptions up to date,
- anything used has been replaced.

A good reminder is when the clocks change. Check your [water and food supply](/en/guides/emergency-water-and-food-supply/) at the same time.
